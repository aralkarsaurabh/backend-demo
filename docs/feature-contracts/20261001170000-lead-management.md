## 2. Business Objective

Let an organization capture sales leads, work them through a pipeline, assign them to its members, and convert a won lead into a Customer in one atomic step.

## 3. User Story

As an organization member, I want to record and work leads and convert a qualified lead into a customer, so that my organization has one place from prospect to customer.

## 4. Actors

Organization roles only (platform `User.role` plays no part, D25):

- `OWNER`, `ADMIN`: every lead action.
- `MEMBER`: every lead action except delete.
- Non-members: no access (they get `404 ORGANIZATION_NOT_FOUND`, D9).
- A platform `ADMIN` has no special access (D10).

## 5. Entry Point

API only: `/api/v1/organizations/:organizationId/leads`. No frontend in this pass.

## 6. UI Reference

N/A. No UI in this pass. The API is the deliverable.

## 7. User Flow

1. A member of an organization creates a lead (`POST`). It starts as `NEW`, unassigned.
2. Members list leads, searching and filtering by status, assignee, source and creation date.
3. A member updates lead data or moves the status among `NEW`, `CONTACTED`, `QUALIFIED`, `UNQUALIFIED`, `LOST` (`PATCH`).
4. A member assigns the lead to another member of the same organization (`PATCH` with `assignedToUserId`, needs `lead:assign`), or unassigns it with `null`.
5. When the lead is won, a member converts it (`POST .../convert`). A Customer is created from the lead and the lead becomes `CONVERTED`, in one transaction.
6. A converted lead is read-only. An `ADMIN`/`OWNER` may still delete it, and the customer is not affected.
7. Failure paths: a lead of another organization is `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND`; converting twice is `409 LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED`; assigning a non-member is `400 ASSIGNED_USER_NOT_MEMBER`; a `MEMBER` deleting is `403`.

## 9. Validation Rules

- Create body (strict, D32): `name` (required), `email`, `phone`, `company`, `source`, `notes`. No `status` or `assignedToUserId` on create; both are set later through `PATCH`.
- Update body (strict, partial, at least one field): `name`, `email`, `phone`, `company`, `source`, `notes`, `status`, `assignedToUserId`. `null` clears a nullable field. `name` cannot be `null`.
- `status: "CONVERTED"` in `PATCH` is a validation error (D30).
- `assignedToUserId` present in `PATCH` requires `lead:assign` in addition to `lead:update`.
- Path ids (`organizationId`, `leadId`) must be uuids; an invalid uuid is `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
- List query: `page` integer >= 1 (default 1); `limit` integer 1-100 (default 20); `search` max 100 chars; `status`, `source` enums; `assignedToUserId` uuid; `createdFrom`/`createdTo` ISO dates (`createdFrom` <= `createdTo`); `sortBy` whitelist `createdAt`, `name`, `company` (default `createdAt`); `sortOrder` `asc`/`desc` (default `desc`). Unknown query keys are rejected.
- Convert has no body (a body with fields is rejected).
- No uniqueness rule on email or phone (D33).

## 10. Business Rules

- D24: A lead belongs to exactly one organization. Every `LeadRepository` method takes `organizationId` and filters on it together with the lead id.
- D25: Access comes from the organization membership, resolved per request by `requireOrganizationMembership()`. The JWT carries no organization data (D5).
- D26: A lead of another organization is `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND`, never 403, so lead ids cannot be probed.
- D27: Permissions live in code: `lead:read`, `lead:create`, `lead:update`, `lead:delete`, `lead:assign`, `lead:convert`. `MEMBER` has all of them except `lead:delete`.
- D28: Hard delete. No `deletedAt`, restore or trash.
- D29: The assignee must be a member of the same organization. The use case checks it with `OrganizationMembershipRepository.findByOrganizationAndUser`. A user that does not exist and a user that is not a member get the same `400 ASSIGNED_USER_NOT_MEMBER`. The check is not a foreign key; a member removed later stays assigned (see Future Improvements).
- D30: Conversion happens only through `POST .../convert`. It is a single repository method (`convert`) that, in one transaction, creates the Customer in the same organization, copies `name`, `email`, `phone`, `company` and `notes` from the lead, and updates the lead to `status = CONVERTED`, `convertedAt = now`, `convertedCustomerId = customer.id`. The lead update is guarded with `WHERE status <> 'CONVERTED'`; if no row matches, the transaction rolls back and the call fails with `LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED`. A lead in any status except `CONVERTED` can be converted.
- D31: A converted lead is read-only. `PATCH` returns `409 LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED`. `update` is also guarded with `WHERE status <> 'CONVERTED'` in SQL, so a concurrent conversion cannot be overwritten. Delete is still allowed.
- D32: The body never carries `organizationId`, `id`, `convertedAt`, `convertedCustomerId`, `createdAt` or `updatedAt`. Bodies are strict.
- D33: Conversion does not look for an existing customer with the same email or phone, so it can create a duplicate. Customers have no uniqueness rule (D21).
- Status moves freely among `NEW`, `CONTACTED`, `QUALIFIED`, `UNQUALIFIED`, `LOST`; there is no enforced pipeline order.
- List order is deterministic: the chosen sort field, then `id`.
- Search is case-insensitive substring over `name`, `email`, `phone`, `company`, runs in PostgreSQL, and escapes `%` and `_`.

## 11. API Contracts

All routes are under `/api/v1/organizations/:organizationId/leads`, require `Authorization: Bearer <access token>`, and use the standard envelope `{ success, message, data, error, meta: { timestamp } }`. Middleware order: `authenticate` → `requireOrganizationMembership` → `requireOrganizationPermission(...)` → controller.

The list response must have exactly the same shape as the customer list response (items key and pagination fields); the examples below show the lead payload only. The implementation copies the customer list response shape and the contract is corrected if the two differ.

### 11.1 Create: `POST /`
- Permission: `lead:create`.
- Body: `{ "name": "Rahul Sharma", "email": "rahul@example.com", "phone": "+919876543210", "company": "ABC Technologies", "source": "WEBSITE", "notes": "Interested in enterprise plan" }`
- `201`, message `Lead created successfully.`, `data.lead` = the lead (status `NEW`, `assignedToUserId: null`, `convertedAt: null`, `convertedCustomerId: null`).
- Failures: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, `401`, `404 ORGANIZATION_NOT_FOUND`, `403 INSUFFICIENT_ORGANIZATION_PERMISSION`.

### 11.2 List: `GET /`
- Permission: `lead:read`.
- Query: see section 9.
- `200`, message `Leads fetched successfully.`, the lead list plus pagination (page, limit, total items, total pages), in the same shape as the customer list.
- Failures: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, `401`, `404 ORGANIZATION_NOT_FOUND`.

### 11.3 Get: `GET /:leadId`
- Permission: `lead:read`.
- `200`, `data.lead`.
- Failures: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR` (bad uuid), `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND`, `404 ORGANIZATION_NOT_FOUND`.

### 11.4 Update: `PATCH /:leadId`
- Permission: `lead:update`; plus `lead:assign` when `assignedToUserId` is in the body.
- Body: partial, e.g. `{ "status": "QUALIFIED", "assignedToUserId": "<uuid>", "notes": "Demo scheduled" }`.
- `200`, `data.lead`.
- Failures: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, `400 ASSIGNED_USER_NOT_MEMBER`, `403 INSUFFICIENT_ORGANIZATION_PERMISSION`, `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND`, `409 LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED`.

### 11.5 Delete: `DELETE /:leadId`
- Permission: `lead:delete` (`ADMIN`, `OWNER`).
- `200`, message `Lead deleted successfully.`, `data: null`.
- Failures: `403 INSUFFICIENT_ORGANIZATION_PERMISSION` (`MEMBER`), `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND`.

### 11.6 Convert: `POST /:leadId/convert`
- Permission: `lead:convert`.
- No body.
- `200`, message `Lead converted successfully.`, `data: { lead, customer }`; `lead` has `status: "CONVERTED"`, `convertedAt` and `convertedCustomerId` set.
- Failures: `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND`, `409 LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED`, `500` with rollback on any other failure (no partial state).

## 12. Backend Processing Flow

Create: authenticate → membership → permission → Zod parse → `CreateLead` → `LeadRepository.create(organizationId, data)` → respond.

List/Get: authenticate → membership → permission → Zod parse → `ListLeads`/`GetLead` → repository (always with `organizationId`) → `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND` when missing → respond.

Update: … → `UpdateLead`: `findById` (404) → if converted, `409` → if `assignedToUserId` is non-null, `AssignLead` check (membership lookup, else `400`) → guarded `update` (null result means it was converted concurrently, so `409`) → respond.

Delete: … → `DeleteLead` → `delete` returns false → `404`.

Convert: … → `ConvertLead`: `findById` (404) → already converted → `409` → `LeadRepository.convert(organizationId, leadId)` (single transaction; guard failure → `LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED`) → respond with lead and customer.

Use cases (one class per operation, no `LeadService`): `CreateLead`, `GetLead`, `ListLeads`, `UpdateLead`, `AssignLead` (the assignee check, called by `UpdateLead`), `DeleteLead`, `ConvertLead`. Controllers validate with `parseOrThrow`; wiring only in `src/app/container.ts`.

## 13. Database Impact

New migration `prisma/migrations/<timestamp>_add_leads/migration.sql` (generated with `prisma migrate diff`, reviewed, applied with `migrate deploy`; never edit an existing migration).

- Enums `LeadStatus`, `LeadSource`.
- Table `Lead` with the section 8 columns.
- FKs: `organizationId` → `Organization` `ON DELETE CASCADE`; `assignedToUserId` → `User` `ON DELETE SET NULL`; `convertedCustomerId` → `Customer` `ON DELETE SET NULL`.
- Unique index on `convertedCustomerId` (a customer comes from at most one lead).
- Indexes, chosen from the real query patterns: `(organizationId, createdAt)` (default list order, date filters), `(organizationId, status)`, `(organizationId, assignedToUserId)`. The `organizationId` prefix is covered by these. No index on `email` or `phone`; search is a scan within the organization, as for customers.
- Transaction boundary: `LeadRepository.convert` only (create Customer + guarded lead update).

## 15. Error Codes

New codes (each added to `ErrorCode`, `ERROR_HTTP_STATUS`, `ERROR_DEFAULT_MESSAGE`, the docs and this contract):

| Code | HTTP | When |
|---|---|---|
| `LEAD_NOT_FOUND` | 404 | No such lead in this organization (including a lead of another organization) |
| `LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED` | 409 | Convert or update on a converted lead |
| `ASSIGNED_USER_NOT_MEMBER` | 400 | `assignedToUserId` is not a member of the organization (or does not exist) |

Existing codes reused: `VALIDATION_ERROR` (400), `INSUFFICIENT_ORGANIZATION_PERMISSION` (403), `ORGANIZATION_NOT_FOUND` (404), `UNAUTHORIZED`, `INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR`.

Not added, on purpose: `INVALID_LEAD_DATA` and `INVALID_LEAD_STATUS` (covered by `VALIDATION_ERROR`), `LEAD_ALREADY_DELETED` (hard delete, so a missing lead is `LEAD_NOT_FOUND`), `LEAD_CONVERSION_FAILED` (a failed conversion rolls back and is an `INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR`).

## 20. Test Cases

Unit tests (`tests/unit`, in-memory fakes, no Prisma):
- `CreateLead`: defaults to `NEW`, unassigned.
- `UpdateLead`: updates fields and status; rejects `409` on a converted lead; assignment to a member succeeds; assignment to a non-member or unknown user is `ASSIGNED_USER_NOT_MEMBER`; `null` unassigns.
- `DeleteLead`: deletes; missing is `LEAD_NOT_FOUND`.
- `ConvertLead`: creates the customer with the copied fields, marks the lead; second call is `LEAD_ALREADY_CONVERTED`; missing is `LEAD_NOT_FOUND`.
- `ListLeads`/`GetLead`: filters, search, order, pagination, not found.
- The fake `LeadRepository.convert` mirrors the rollback behaviour so the use-case tests are meaningful.

HTTP integration (`tests/integration`, real app and Postgres), one test per route for the happy path and each failure above, plus:
- Validation: bad uuid, empty name, bad email, unknown field, `status: CONVERTED` in `PATCH`, `limit` over 100, `createdFrom` after `createdTo`, unknown sort field and unknown query key.
- Permissions: `MEMBER` delete is 403; non-member gets 404 on every route; a platform `ADMIN` who is not a member gets 404.
- Tenant isolation: Org A cannot read, update, delete or convert an Org B lead (all `404 LEAD_NOT_FOUND`); Org A cannot assign a lead to an Org B member (`400 ASSIGNED_USER_NOT_MEMBER`); `organizationId` in the body is rejected.
- Search and filters: search matches each of the four fields, `%` and `_` are literal, filters combine, ties in `createdAt` are ordered by `id` with no overlap or gap across pages.
- Conversion: the customer exists in the same organization with the copied fields; `convertedCustomerId` is set; converting twice is 409 and creates only one customer; converted lead cannot be updated but can be deleted by an `ADMIN`; deleting the customer leaves the lead `CONVERTED` with `convertedCustomerId: null`.

Real-SQL repository tests (`tests/integration/lead-repositories.test.ts`), each proving its guard fails when the guard is removed:
- Every method filters by `organizationId` (cross-organization lookup, update, delete and convert find nothing).
- `update` is guarded by `status <> 'CONVERTED'`.
- `convert` is guarded by `status <> 'CONVERTED'`.
- `convert` rolls back the Customer when the lead update fails (no orphan customer, no `CONVERTED` lead without a customer).
- Search escapes `%` and `_`; list order ends in `id`.

## 23. Future Improvements

Out of scope for this pass:

- Reassigning or unassigning leads when a member is removed from the organization.
- A lead status history or audit trail, soft delete and restore.
- Duplicate detection against existing customers or leads, and a uniqueness rule on email or phone.
- Enforced pipeline transitions and a convert-from-status policy (for example, only `QUALIFIED`).
- Bulk import, bulk assignment, and lead activity or notes timeline.
- Notifications on assignment or conversion.
- Trigram or full-text search index; cursor pagination.
- Rate limiting (applies to the whole API).
